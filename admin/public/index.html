    How publishing works

    1. Photos and videos

    Uploads go straight to Cloudinary, into a folder for the event. Their links are saved into the event in the order shown in the editor.

    2. Saving

    Saving an event or registration updates the website automatically. Changes appear on the live site in about 2–3 minutes.

    3. If two people edit at once

    If someone else saved the same event after you opened it, your save is stopped instead of overwriting their work. Reload the page and make your change again.
